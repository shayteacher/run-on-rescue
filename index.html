Writing

What is a run-on sentence?

Two or more ideas stuck together with no stop.

It was the best of times, it was the worst of times, it was the age of wisdom, it was the age of foolishness, it was the epoch of belief, it was the epoch of incredulity, it was the season of Light, it was the season of Darkness, it was the spring of hope, it was the winter of despair... Charles Dickens, A Tale of Two Cities (1859)

Which detail do you remember?

Too many ideas, no stops. The reader can't decide what to hold on to.

Writing

"But" is the most important word

Before "but"

Not important.

After "but"

That's the point.

Example 1 I like her, but...
The point

...she always eats my snacks.

Example 2 We stayed up late and had a great time. But...
The point

...we had a test at 9 a.m.

Writing

Why do we start with "But"?

Korean

그런데...

"So..." "By the way..." "Anyway..." The story keeps going.

English

But...

"Here comes the problem." Everyone waits for bad news.

The test:

  • Is a problem or a surprise coming? Use "But."
  • No problem? No surprise? Delete it.

Writing

Transitions are a crutch

Firstly,Moreover,In addition,Furthermore,Also,
  • Is it something new? Don't use it.
  • Not sure? Don't use it.
  • Have to justify it? Don't use it.

One transition per paragraph. Maximum.

Free inside a sentence:

andbutsobecause

"But it happened in that order!" The reader already knows. Your sentence order shows the time order.

Writing + Speaking

One sentence, one breath.

Read it out loud in one breath. Did you struggle?

Example 1 I went to the library to study for my test and then my friend came and also she brought snacks so we ate them and then we talked for an hour and we did not study at all.
Break it up

I went to the library to study for my test.

My friend came with snacks.

We ate them and talked for an hour.

We did not study at all.

Example 2 Moreover, I got a new puppy last week and he is very cute and then he chewed my shoes and also he cried all night so nobody in my family could sleep.
Break it up

I got a new puppy last week.

He is very cute.

He chewed my shoes.

He cried all night, so nobody in my family could sleep.
